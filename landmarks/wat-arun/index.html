Wat Arun facts

Bangkok · 82 m tall
82m
the central spire

Its central tower, the prang, rises about 82 metres.

1851
completed

The central prang was finished in 1851, after building began under King Rama II and went on under King Rama III.

Its tiers are covered in broken porcelain and seashells. Some of the china came to Bangkok as ballast in ships from China.

The street that leads there: Khao San Road

The rice street

410m
long

Khao San Road is only about 410 metres long.

Its name means 'milled rice': by the end of the 1800s this district was the biggest rice market in the country.

Rice came up the Chao Phraya River on barges and was unloaded at a canal nearby.
