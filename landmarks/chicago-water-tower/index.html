Chicago Water Tower facts

Chicago · 47 m tall
1869
built

The Water Tower was finished in 1869 to hide a tall iron pipe that helped pump water to the city.

1871
the Great Fire

It was the only public building in the burned area to survive the Great Chicago Fire of 1871.

47m
tall

It stands 47 metres tall, built of yellow limestone.

The street that leads there: Michigan Avenue

North of the river

This stretch of Michigan Avenue runs about a mile north from the Chicago River, lined with shops, hotels and towers.

It was once a narrow road called Pine Street. It grew into a grand avenue after a double-decked bridge opened over the river in 1920.

1920
the bridge

That bridge is a bascule bridge: its two halves tip up to let tall boats through.
