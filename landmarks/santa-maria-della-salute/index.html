Santa Maria della Salute facts

Venice
1631
begun

Venice vowed to build it in 1630, in thanks for the end of a terrible plague. Work began in 1631.

56years
to build

It was finished in 1687. Its architect, Baldassare Longhena, died five years before.

Every year on 21 November a bridge on floats is laid across the Grand Canal, so people can walk to it.

The long quay

1km
of quay

The Zattere runs for about a kilometre along the Giudecca Canal, to the point where it meets the basin of San Marco.

Its name means rafts: salt, timber and coal were once floated down the canal on rafts and landed here.

400gondolas
today

In the 1500s Venice had about 10,000 gondolas and boats like them. Today there are about 400 gondolas.
