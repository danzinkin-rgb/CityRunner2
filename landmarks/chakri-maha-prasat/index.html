Chakri Maha Prasat facts

Bangkok
1882
completed

The throne hall was finished in 1882, a hundred years after the Grand Palace was founded.

Its body is built in a European style, but its three roofs are traditional Thai, each crowned with a tall spire.

1782
palace founded

The Grand Palace was begun in 1782, the year Bangkok became the capital.

Life by the canal

Bangkok was once called the 'Venice of the East', because canals, called khlongs, crossed the whole city.

1,682
canals

In 2019 the city counted 1,682 canals, with 2,604 kilometres of waterways between them.

Many canals were filled in to make roads, but in places like Bang Luang, wooden houses still stand right on the water.
