Ulugh Beg Observatory facts

Samarkand
1420
founded

Ulugh Beg, Timur's grandson and an astronomer, built his observatory here around 1420.

40m
sextant radius

Its great sextant, a curved track of marble set into the hill, had a radius of about 40 metres.

1908
found again

The observatory was lost for centuries, until an archaeologist found its remains in 1908.
