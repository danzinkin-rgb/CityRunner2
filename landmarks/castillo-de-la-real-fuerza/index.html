Castillo de la Real Fuerza facts

Havana
1577
completed

Completed in 1577, it is one of the oldest stone forts in the Americas.

The bronze weathervane on its tower, La Giraldilla, was cast in the 1630s and has become a symbol of Havana.

The Giraldilla on the tower today is a copy; the original is kept safe inside.

The sea wall

1901
begun

Building of the Malecón began in 1901, to protect the city from the sea.

7km
of sea wall

It runs for about seven kilometres along the shore, built in stages over more than fifty years.

In rough weather, waves break right over the wall and across the road.
