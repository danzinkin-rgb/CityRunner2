Blue Mosque facts

Istanbul
1616
completed

Built from 1609 to 1616 for Sultan Ahmed I, it is still a working mosque.

6minarets
around it

It has six minarets, which was very unusual when it was built.

20,000tiles
inside

More than 20,000 hand-painted tiles from İznik line its walls inside, many of them blue: that is how it got its nickname.
