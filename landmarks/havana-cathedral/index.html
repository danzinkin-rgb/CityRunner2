Havana Cathedral facts

Havana
1777
completed

Begun by the Jesuits in 1748, it was finished in 1777.

Its two bell towers are different widths, so that rainwater could drain from the square beside it.

1789
made a cathedral

It became Havana's cathedral in 1789.

The street that leads there: Calle Obispo

The bishop's street

Calle Obispo goes back to the 1500s, one of the oldest streets in Old Havana.

It has had many names, among them Pi Margall from 1905, but in 1936 it went back to Obispo, 'bishop'.

1982
World Heritage

Old Havana and its forts were listed by UNESCO in 1982.
