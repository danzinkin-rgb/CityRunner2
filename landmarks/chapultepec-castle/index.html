Chapultepec Castle facts

Mexico City
1785
building began

Construction started in 1785, on a hill where Aztec rulers once had a retreat.

1847
the cadets who defended it

During a battle here in 1847, a group of young military cadets are remembered in Mexico as the Niños Héroes for standing their ground.

It later became home to Mexican presidents, and today it holds the National History Museum.

The street that leads there: Xochimilco

The floating gardens

170km
of canals

Xochimilco's canals stretch about 170 kilometres — nearly all that survives of the lake the Aztecs once sailed.

1987
named a World Heritage Site

UNESCO protects Xochimilco's floating gardens alongside the historic centre of Mexico City.

The gardens are built on chinampas — islands woven from lake mud and reeds — and farmers still grow flowers on them today.
