The Old Fort facts

Zanzibar
1699
built by

The fort was built by about 1699, after Oman took the island from Portugal, on the site of a Portuguese church.

It has served as a garrison, a prison and a railway terminus.

Today it is a cultural centre, and home to the Zanzibar International Film Festival.

The carved doors

2000
World Heritage

UNESCO listed Stone Town in 2000 for its mix of African, Arab, Persian, Indian and European building.

Its houses are famous for their carved wooden doors, many studded with brass.

Doors in the Indian style have rounded tops; those in the Omani Arab style are square.
