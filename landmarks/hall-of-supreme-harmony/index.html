Hall of Supreme Harmony facts

Beijing · 35 m tall
35m
tall

With its marble terrace, the hall rises about 35 metres.

1695
rebuilt

First built in the 1420s, the hall standing today was rebuilt in 1695.

It is one of the largest wooden buildings in China. Emperors held their grandest ceremonies here.

Some of the oldest shops in Beijing stand here, known for roast duck, tea, silk, shoes and hats.
