Turtle Tower facts

Hanoi

The little tower was built on its island in the lake in 1886.

It is named for the golden turtle of the lake's legend.

Visitors cannot go out to it: everyone sees it from the shore.

The street that leads there: Hang Gai

The silk street

Hanoi's Old Quarter is called the 36 Streets. Each street was named after the trade of the guild that worked there.

'Hang' means goods. Hang Gai was a centre of the silk trade, and it is still known for silk and tailors.

The number 36 is not exact: the Old Quarter has more streets than that today.
