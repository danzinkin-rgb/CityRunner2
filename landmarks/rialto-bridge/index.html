Rialto Bridge facts

Venice
1591
completed

The stone bridge was built from 1588 to 1591, to a design by Antonio da Ponte.

It replaced a wooden bridge and kept its shape: one arch, with rows of shops along it.

It is the oldest of the four bridges across the Grand Canal.

The street that leads there: Strada Nova

The new street

1871
opened

The Strada Nova, the "new street", opened in 1871, cut through old houses to make one walk from the Rialto to the railway station.

Venice is built on 126 islands, joined by 472 bridges.

There are no cars in the old city. Goods go round on porters' trolleys, and by boat.
