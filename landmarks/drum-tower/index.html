Drum Tower facts

Beijing · 46.7 m tall
47m
tall

The Drum Tower stands about 47 metres tall.

1272
first built

It was first built in 1272, and rebuilt on this spot in 1420.

Drums were beaten here to tell the city the time. It once held 25 drums; one great drum is left.

The street that leads there: Nanluoguxiang

A hutong lane

700years
old

This lane was laid out more than 700 years ago, when Kublai Khan made Beijing his capital.

It was named for its shape: it is higher in the middle than at either end, like a hunched back ('luoguo').

Lanes like this, called hutongs, run between the walls of old courtyard houses.
