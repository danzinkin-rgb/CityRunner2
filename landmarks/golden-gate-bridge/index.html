Golden Gate Bridge facts

San Francisco · 227 m tall
227m
tower height

Each of its two towers rises 227 m above the water.

1937
opened

When it opened in 1937 it was the longest suspension bridge in the world.

Painters work on it all year round to protect the steel from the salty sea air. The colour is called International Orange.

The street that leads there: Lombard Street

The crooked street

8bends
in one block

Lombard Street squeezes eight hairpin bends into a single steep block on Russian Hill.

1922
the bends were built

The bends were added in 1922 because the hill was too steep to drive straight down.

The road is paved in red brick and lined with flowerbeds on every bend.
