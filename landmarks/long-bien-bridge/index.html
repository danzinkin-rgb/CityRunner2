Long Bien Bridge facts

Hanoi
1903
opened

Designed by the Paris firm Daydé & Pillé, it opened in 1903 as the first steel bridge across the Red River.

2,290m
long

It is 2,290 metres long and crosses the river on 19 spans.

It was first named after Paul Doumer, the French governor-general, and was renamed Long Bien after 1954.

Houses beside the rails

1902
railway built

The railway through here was built in 1902, and trains still run along it.

The houses stand so close to the rails that people step back into their doorways when a train goes by.

In 2019 the cafés along the tracks were closed to visitors for safety. They reopened in 2023 under stricter rules.
