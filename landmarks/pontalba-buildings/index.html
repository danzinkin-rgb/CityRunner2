Pontalba Buildings facts

New Orleans
1851
completed

Baroness Micaela Almonester de Pontalba built the two matching red-brick rows from 1849 to 1851.

Her initials, AP, are worked into the cast-iron railings of the balconies.

They face each other across Jackson Square, with shops below and homes above.

The parade route

1835
streetcar

The St. Charles streetcar has run since 1835: the oldest streetcar line still running in the world.

It is one of the main Mardi Gras parade routes, lined with mansions and live oaks planted in the early 1900s.

1857
the first floats

In 1857 the Mistick Krewe of Comus brought torch-lit processions and themed floats to Mardi Gras.
