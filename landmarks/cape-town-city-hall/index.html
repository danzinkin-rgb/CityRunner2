Cape Town City Hall facts

Cape Town
1905
opened

Its cornerstone was laid in 1900, and the hall opened in 1905.

39bells
in its tower

Its clock tower holds a carillon of 39 bells, one of the largest in South Africa.

On 11 February 1990, Nelson Mandela gave his first speech after his release from prison from its balcony.

The working harbour

1860
harbour begun

Prince Alfred, Queen Victoria's son, tipped the first load of stone for the harbour's breakwater in 1860.

The Waterfront is named after both of them: Victoria and Alfred.

It is still a working harbour, with fishing boats and tugs moored among the shops and restaurants.
