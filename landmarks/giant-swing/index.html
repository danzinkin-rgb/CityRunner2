Giant Swing facts

Bangkok · 21.15 m tall
21m
tall

The Giant Swing stands 21 metres tall, in front of the Devasathan shrine.

1784
first built

It was first built in 1784. Its great teak pillars were replaced, to the same design, in 2007.

A swinging ceremony was once held here every year. It stopped in 1935.

The street that leads there: Yaowarat Road

Bangkok's Chinatown

1.5km
long

Yaowarat Road runs about one and a half kilometres through the heart of Bangkok's Chinatown.

1891
ordered

King Chulalongkorn ordered the road built in 1891, over land that had been rice fields and canals.

It bends because it was laid out around the homes already there. People say it looks like the body of a dragon.
