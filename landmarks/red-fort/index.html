Red Fort facts

Emperor Shah Jahan's fort took about nine years to build, from 1639 to 1648.

2.4km
of walls

Its red sandstone walls run for about 2.4 kilometres, and give the Red Fort its name.

The main entrance, the Lahori Gate, is named after the city of Lahore, which it faces.

The street that leads there: Chandni Chowk

The moonlit square

1650
laid out

Princess Jahanara, daughter of Emperor Shah Jahan, designed the market in 1650, leading up to the gates of the Red Fort.

Its name means 'moonlit square': a pool in the middle of the street once reflected the moon.

A canal once ran down the centre of the street. Today it is one of the biggest wholesale markets in India.
