Temple of Literature facts

Hanoi
1070
founded

The temple was founded in 1070, and in 1076 Vietnam's first national university opened beside it.

82tablets
on stone turtles

Eighty-two stone tablets, each carried on the back of a stone turtle, record the scholars who passed the royal exams.

1805
the pavilion

Its Constellation of Literature Pavilion was built in 1805, and it has become a symbol of Hanoi.

The street that leads there: Hoan Kiem Lake

The lake of the returned sword

Hoan Kiem means 'lake of the returned sword'.

1428
the legend

The story goes that in 1428 a golden turtle rose from the lake and took back the magic sword Emperor Le Loi had used to win his war.

Before the legend, the lake was called Luc Thuy, 'green water'.
