Coit Tower facts

San Francisco · 64 m tall
1933
completed

Coit Tower was finished in 1933, paid for with money left to the city by Lillie Hitchcock Coit.

64m
tall

The tower is 64 m tall and stands on top of Telegraph Hill.

Inside, the walls are covered in murals painted by more than 25 artists in 1934.

The street that leads there: Market Street

The city's main street

4.8km
end to end

Market Street runs about 4.8 km from the Ferry Building on the bay towards Twin Peaks.

1847
laid out

It was laid out in 1847, the year the town changed its name from Yerba Buena to San Francisco.

Its historic streetcars were collected from cities all over the world, and still carry passengers.
