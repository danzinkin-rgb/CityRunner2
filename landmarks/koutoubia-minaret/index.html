Koutoubia Minaret facts

Marrakech · 77 m tall
77m
tall, with its spire

The minaret rises 77 metres, spire and all, and can be seen from almost anywhere in the old city.

1195
finished around

It was completed around 1195 under Caliph Yaqub al-Mansur, and it has stood almost unchanged ever since.

Its name means the booksellers' mosque: booksellers once kept their stalls all around it.

The square that never sleeps

2001
honoured by UNESCO

In 2001 UNESCO named the square's storytellers, musicians and performers a Masterpiece of the Oral and Intangible Heritage of Humanity, one of the very first.

1985
World Heritage Site

The old walled city around the square, the medina, has been a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1985.

Storytellers still gather their listeners in a circle called a halqa and tell tales that have been passed on by word of mouth for centuries.
