Qutub Minar facts

Delhi · 72.5 m tall
72.5m
tall

At 72.5 metres, it is the tallest minaret in the world built of brick.

1199
begun

Qutb ud-Din Aibak began the tower in 1199, and his successor Iltutmish added three more storeys.

It narrows from 14.3 metres across at the base to just 2.7 metres at the top.

The street that leads there: Connaught Place

Rings of white colonnades

1933
completed

Built between 1929 and 1933, it was named after the Duke of Connaught, a son of Queen Victoria.

Architect Robert Tor Russell set its white colonnades in rings around a central park.

The design was modelled on the Royal Crescent, a curving terrace of houses in the English city of Bath.
