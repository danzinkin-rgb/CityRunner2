The Registan facts

Samarkand
1420
first madrasa

Ulugh Beg's madrasa, on the left, was built from 1417 to 1420, with fifty rooms for its students.

Sher-Dor's gate shows two tigers with rising suns on their backs, rare in buildings of its kind for showing living creatures.

1660
Tilya-Kori finished

Tilya-Kori, at the back, was built from 1646 to 1660. Its name means 'gilded'.

The street that leads there: Registan Square

The sandy place

Registan means 'sandy place'. It was the heart of the old city, where people gathered to hear announcements.

1660
last madrasa finished

Its three madrasas, or schools, were built over more than two centuries, from 1417 to 1660.

All three face the square, each behind a great tiled gate.
