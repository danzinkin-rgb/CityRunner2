The street that leads there: Divanyolu

The road to the council

Divanyolu follows the Mese, the main street laid out by Emperor Constantine in the 300s.

Under the Ottomans it led to the Divan, the imperial council at Topkapı Palace, and that gave it its name.

At its start stood the Milion, the marker from which distances across the empire were measured.
