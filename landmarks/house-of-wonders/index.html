House of Wonders facts

Zanzibar
1883
built

Sultan Barghash built it in 1883 as a palace for ceremonies.

It was the first building in Zanzibar with electricity, and the first in East Africa with a lift: that is how it got its name.

Part of it collapsed in 2020, and it is being restored.

The street that leads there: Forodhani Gardens

The seafront

Forodhani means 'customs': the gardens lie by the island's old customs house.

Every evening the gardens become an open-air food market, with grilled seafood and sugar-cane juice.

The old palaces of the sultans line the seafront here, looking out over the harbour.
