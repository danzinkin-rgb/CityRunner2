Palace of the Congress facts

Buenos Aires · 80 m tall
80m
to the dome

Its green copper dome rises 80 metres.

1906
opened

The palace opened in 1906, although the work was not fully finished until 1946.

Its architect, Vittorio Meano, also worked on the Teatro Colón.

The wide avenue

140m
wide

The avenue is about 140 metres wide: it takes up a whole city block.

It is often called the widest avenue in the world.

1937
building began

Work began in 1937, and the whole avenue was not finished until 1980.
