Willis Tower facts

Chicago · 442 m tall
442m
tall

The tower rises 442 metres to its roof, more with its antennas.

1973
opened

When it opened in 1973 it was the tallest building in the world, and it held that title for nearly 25 years.

It is built as nine square tubes bundled together. They stop at different heights, so the tower steps back as it rises.

The street that leads there: Wacker Drive

Along the river

Wacker Drive runs along the Chicago River, and has a second road on a lower level beneath it.

37bridges
that move

Chicago has 37 bridges that open to let boats through, by most counts more than any other city in the world.

Most are bascule bridges, a design so common here that it is called the 'Chicago type'.
