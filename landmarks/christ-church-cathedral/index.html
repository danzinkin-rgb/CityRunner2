Christ Church Cathedral facts

Zanzibar
1873
slave market closed

The cathedral stands where the island's slave market was, closed in 1873. Its first stone was laid that Christmas.

Bishop Edward Steere, who campaigned against slavery, led the building, and designed its unusual barrel-vaulted roof.

Its altar is said to stand where the whipping post was, so that the place of suffering became a place of worship.

The main market

1904
market hall built

The covered market hall was built in 1904. People here call it Marikiti Kuu, 'the main market'.

Its stalls sell spices, fresh fish, fruit, and almost everything else.

The dala dalas, small trucks turned into buses, leave for the rest of the island from beside it.
