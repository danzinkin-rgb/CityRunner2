Waterfront Clock Tower facts

Cape Town
1882
built

Finished in 1882, the tower was the harbour's first Port Captain's office.

Upstairs, mirrors let the Port Captain watch the whole harbour. Downstairs, a tide gauge showed ships how high the water was.

When it was restored in the 1990s, layers of grey paint were stripped off to find its original red.

The street that leads there: Bo-Kaap

The painted hillside

1763
first houses

Jan de Waal began building houses to rent here in 1763. One of the first, on Wale Street, is now the Bo-Kaap Museum.

It has more buildings from before 1850 than anywhere else in South Africa.

Its houses are painted in bright colours. Many people say they were first painted to celebrate freedom when slavery ended.
