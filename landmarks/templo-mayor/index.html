Templo Mayor facts

Mexico City · 45 m tall
1325
construction began

Building started here soon after 1325, when the Mexica people founded their city on an island in a lake.

7layers
built again and again

Each ruler expanded the temple by building a new layer over the old one — archaeologists found seven layers stacked inside.

It was rediscovered by accident in 1978, when electrical workers dug up a huge carved stone disc nearby.

The street that leads there: Paseo de la Reforma

A boulevard built for an emperor

15km
end to end

Paseo de la Reforma runs fifteen kilometres across the city, from the historic centre to Chapultepec park.

1865
first laid out

Emperor Maximilian ordered the avenue built to link his palace downtown with his home at Chapultepec Castle.

Its grand traffic circles, called glorietas, were added decades later, under President Porfirio Díaz.
