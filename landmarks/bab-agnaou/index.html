Bab Agnaou facts

Marrakech
1188
built around

Caliph Yaqub al-Mansur built the gate around 1188 as the grand entrance to his royal kasbah.

Most of the city's gates are rammed red earth, but Bab Agnaou is cut stone, carved with an inscription and flowers around its horseshoe arch.

It once had two towers and a covered passage behind it. Those are gone, and the carved front is what still stands.

The street that leads there: The Souks

A maze of markets

Souk Semmarine, the main lane of the souks, is roofed with a long iron trellis that keeps the shops in shade.

In the dyers' souk, freshly dyed wool is hung across the alleys to dry in bright streamers of colour.

Each part of the souks has its own trade: one for wool in the morning, one for spices and remedies, one for slippers, one for lanterns.
