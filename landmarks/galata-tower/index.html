Galata Tower facts

Istanbul · 66.9 m tall
67m
to the tip

The tower stands about 67 metres to the tip of its cap.

1348
built

Genoese merchants built it in 1348, and called it the Tower of Christ.

A storm blew its cap off in 1875. The pointed cap was put back when the tower was restored in the 1960s.

The tram street

1.4km
long

İstiklal Avenue runs about 1.4 kilometres, from Taksim Square down to Tünel.

1871
first trams

Trams have run along here since 1871, pulled by horses until electric trams came in 1914.

Today a little red heritage tram still rattles down the middle of the street.
