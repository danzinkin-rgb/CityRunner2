# Buenos Aires: facts for review

**DRAFT. Nothing here ships until Dan has approved it.** The facts are in `src/facts.js` already, marked DRAFT, so the city can be played and tested; the city is in the release queue and no player sees it.

3 streets × 3 facts, 3 monuments × 3 facts.

## Approach

- Facts are architectural and historical. No politics: the Casa Rosada is described as the President's office and nothing more, and the Falklands/Malvinas are not mentioned.
- **The Obelisco is not in the game, not even on the skyline.** It was built in 1936 by Alberto Prebisch, who died in 1970; Argentina protects works for life + 70, so it is protected until 2041.
- Caminito's colours: the street as a whole is shown in its general style (bright tin houses), not as a copy of any one painted house.

## Streets

### 1. Caminito: *a street museum*

| Statistic | Fact |
|---|---|
| **1959**, street museum | Caminito opened as an open-air street museum in 1959. |
| *(none)* | Its houses are conventillos, homes of wood and sheet metal built by immigrants, many from Genoa, in the late 1800s. |
| *(none)* | The painter Benito Quinquela Martín led the work to bring the lane back to life and cover it in colour. |

### 2. Avenida de Mayo: *the grand avenue*

| Statistic | Fact |
|---|---|
| **1894**, opened | Avenida de Mayo opened in 1894, running from the Plaza de Mayo, in front of the President's palace, to the Congress. |
| **1858**, Café Tortoni | Café Tortoni, on this avenue, has served coffee since 1858, and was among the first cafés in the city to put tables out on the pavement. |
| *(none)* | Its tall fronts, domes and balconies were built to look like the grand boulevards of Europe. |

Café Tortoni is a real business: naming it in a fact is description, and its name and look appear nowhere in the game itself.

### 3. Avenida 9 de Julio: *the wide avenue*

| Statistic | Fact |
|---|---|
| **140 m**, wide | The avenue is about 140 metres wide: it takes up a whole city block. |
| *(none)* | It is often called the widest avenue in the world. |
| **1937**, building began | Work began in 1937, and the whole avenue was not finished until 1980. |

Width: most sources give 140 m; Guinness gives about 110 m and has questioned the "widest" title, so the fact says "often called".
