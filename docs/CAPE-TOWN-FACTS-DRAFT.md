## Approach

- Facts are architectural and historical.
- Two facts touch on hard history, and both are your call:
  - **Bo-Kaap's colours.** The common account is that residents painted their houses bright colours to celebrate freedom when slavery ended. Tourism sources tell it; Wikipedia does not. The draft says "Many people say".
  - **Nelson Mandela's speech from the City Hall balcony** (11 February 1990, his first after release). It is the building's most famous moment. The fact states it plainly with no comment.
- Robben Island is left out.

## Copyright

- The Castle (1666–79) and the Clock Tower (1882): centuries old.
- City Hall (1905) is by Harry Austin Reid and Frederick George Green, who won the design competition in the 1890s. **Their death dates were not found.** South Africa's term is life + 50, so it would only be protected if one of them lived past 1975, which for architects already practising in the 1890s is very unlikely. The UK would be life + 70 (past 1955). The US does not protect pre-1990 buildings. Worth a final check, the same way Chapultepec's architects were checked for Mexico.
