### Data safety

**Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? No.**

Play then asks no more questions about data types. Reasoning, so it can be
re-checked:

- Google's definition: data is *collected* when it is transmitted off the
  device by the app or by any library in it. Scores, nickname, progress and
  the random ID stay in on-device storage (COMPLIANCE §1.2).
- Purchases: Google's guidance says data collected by a payment service such
  as Google Play's billing system does not have to be declared when the app
  does not access the financial data and the service collects it directly.
  Both hold. The app learns only which product is owned and keeps that on
  the device.
- Ratings: the rating card is Google Play's own (the in-app review
  library). Google's documentation lists what it handles as "user-entered
  data (rating and free-text review)", typed by the player into Play's card
  and sent by Play, never seen by the app. Same reasoning as billing: the
  service collects it directly. Google adds that the developer is
  "solely responsible" for the Data safety answer, so this is the second
  answer to revisit if Google's guidance changes.
- No other library sends anything. The Android project has no Firebase, no
  Play Services SDK and no analytics; `test/android.mjs` fails if one appears
  in the Gradle files, or if any Google Play library other than the review
  one does.
- Android backup: the app keeps Android's default `allowBackup`, so progress
  can come back on a new phone through the player's own Google account
  backup. That copy goes to the user's own account and never reaches the
  developer. Google's Data safety article does not mention Android backup
  directly; this is the one answer to revisit if Google adds guidance.
  Turning backup off (`android:allowBackup="false"`) would remove the
  question at the cost of progress not surviving a phone change.

**When this changes:** the day online leaderboards ship (COMPLIANCE §2.1),
the answer becomes "Yes": App activity › Other actions (scores) and App
info and performance, plus the random ID under Device or other IDs, all
"collected, not shared", "processed ephemerally: no", "required", purposes
App functionality and Analytics, encrypted in transit, deletion through
Erase my data.
