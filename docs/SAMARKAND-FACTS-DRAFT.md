# Samarkand: facts for review

**Approved by Dan, 13 October 2026**, including the points under "To check before approval" below, which he checked and accepted. The facts are in `src/facts.js`; the city is in the release queue and no player sees it until it is released.

3 streets × 3 facts, 3 monuments × 3 facts.

## Approach

- Facts are architectural and historical. Timur's conquests are left out; he appears as a builder and as the man buried in Gur-e-Amir.
- **Monument change from the plan:** the Bibi-Khanym Mosque was swapped for the **Ulugh Beg Observatory**. Bibi-Khanym's dome and much of the building were reconstructed from 1974 onwards, which leaves its copyright position less clear. The observatory (1420s) is clear, and it is a different kind of building from the other two.

## Monuments

No height graphic for any of the three: their facts carry the page.

### Ulugh Beg Observatory

| Statistic | Fact |
|---|---|
| **1420**, founded | Ulugh Beg, Timur's grandson and an astronomer, built his observatory here around 1420. |
| **40 m**, sextant radius | Its great sextant, a curved track of marble set into the hill, had a radius of about 40 metres. |
| **1908**, found again | The observatory was lost for centuries, until an archaeologist found its remains in 1908. |

The monument shows the observatory as it is thought to have stood: a round three-storey building, about 46 m across, with the sextant inside. Only the underground part of the sextant survives today.

## Copyright

All three are Timurid or later work of the 1400s to 1600s. Restorations of the Registan and Gur-e-Amir followed the old designs.
