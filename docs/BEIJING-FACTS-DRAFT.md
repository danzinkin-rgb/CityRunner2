# Beijing: facts for review

**DRAFT. Nothing here ships until Dan has approved it.** The facts are in `src/facts.js` already, marked DRAFT, so the city can be played and tested; the city is in the release queue and no player sees it.

3 streets × 3 facts, 3 monuments × 3 facts.

## Approach

- Facts are architectural and historical, about buildings from the 1200s to the 1600s. Nothing about modern politics.
- **Tiananmen Square and its monuments are not in the game**, not even on the skyline.
- Wangfujing's brief renaming during the Cultural Revolution is left out.
- Note for the store: selling a game in mainland China needs a government licence, whatever its content. That is a store question, not one for this city's content.

## Monuments

### Drum Tower (scale: 46.7 m, compared with a bus)

| Statistic | Fact |
|---|---|
| **47 m**, tall | The Drum Tower stands about 47 metres tall. |
| **1272**, first built | It was first built in 1272, and rebuilt on this spot in 1420. |
| *(none)* | Drums were beaten here to tell the city the time. It once held 25 drums; one great drum is left. |

### Hall of Prayer for Good Harvests (scale: 38 m, compared with a bus)

| Statistic | Fact |
|---|---|
| **38 m**, tall | The hall is round, built of wood, and 38 metres tall. |
| **1889**, struck by lightning | First built in 1420, it burned after a lightning strike in 1889 and was rebuilt to the same design. |
| *(none)* | Emperors came here every year to pray for a good harvest. |

### Hall of Supreme Harmony (scale: 35 m, compared with a bus)

| Statistic | Fact |
|---|---|
| **35 m**, tall | With its marble terrace, the hall rises about 35 metres. |
| **1695**, rebuilt | First built in the 1420s, the hall standing today was rebuilt in 1695. |
| *(none)* | It is one of the largest wooden buildings in China. Emperors held their grandest ceremonies here. |

Height: about 27 m for the hall alone, 35 m with its terrace; the fact says "with its marble terrace". Wikipedia says "one of the largest" wooden buildings; some travel sites say "the largest", which is not well supported.

## To check before approval

- "Like a hunched back ('luoguo')": the name is explained by the lane's shape in the sources; the translation is mine.
- Qianmen's old shops "known for roast duck, tea, silk, shoes and hats": general knowledge of its famous old shops.

## Copyright

All three are Ming buildings, rebuilt to their old designs (the Hall of Prayer after 1889, the Hall of Supreme Harmony in 1695): no copyright question.
