# Istanbul: facts for review

**DRAFT. Nothing here ships until Dan has approved it.** The facts are in `src/facts.js` already, marked DRAFT, so the city can be played and tested; the city is in the release queue and no player sees it.

3 streets × 3 facts, 3 monuments × 3 facts.

## Approach

- Facts are architectural and historical.
- Hagia Sophia and the Blue Mosque are working mosques today. They are shown as buildings, from outside, as the Dome of the Rock is in Jerusalem.
- **Hagia Sophia's changes of status are given as dates only**: "Built as a cathedral, it became a mosque in 1453, a museum in 1934, and a mosque again in 2020." No comment on any of them. Say if you would rather leave the 2020 date out.

## Streets

### 1. İstiklal Avenue: *the tram street*

| Statistic | Fact |
|---|---|
| **1.4 km**, long | İstiklal Avenue runs about 1.4 kilometres, from Taksim Square down to Tünel. |
| **1871**, first trams | Trams have run along here since 1871, pulled by horses until electric trams came in 1914. |
| *(none)* | Today a little red heritage tram still rattles down the middle of the street. |

### 2. Grand Bazaar: *a city under one roof*

| Statistic | Fact |
|---|---|
| **1455**, begun | Sultan Mehmed II began the Grand Bazaar around 1455. |
| **61 streets**, under its roofs | About 61 covered streets run through it, lined with thousands of shops. |
| *(none)* | In 1890 a count found 4,399 shops at work here. |

### 3. Divanyolu: *the road to the council*

| Statistic | Fact |
|---|---|
| *(none)* | Divanyolu follows the Mese, the main street laid out by Emperor Constantine in the 300s. |
| *(none)* | Under the Ottomans it led to the Divan, the imperial council at Topkapı Palace, and that gave it its name. |
| *(none)* | At its start stood the Milion, the marker from which distances across the empire were measured. |

## Monuments

### Galata Tower (scale: 66.9 m, compared with a bus)

| Statistic | Fact |
|---|---|
| **67 m**, to the tip | The tower stands about 67 metres to the tip of its cap. |
| **1348**, built | Genoese merchants built it in 1348, and called it the Tower of Christ. |
| *(none)* | A storm blew its cap off in 1875. The pointed cap was put back when the tower was restored in the 1960s. |

Height: 66.9 m with the ornament on top, 62.6 m without; some sources say nearly 70 m.

### Blue Mosque (no sourced height: shows altitude, 39 m)

| Statistic | Fact |
|---|---|
| **1616**, completed | Built from 1609 to 1616 for Sultan Ahmed I, it is still a working mosque. |
| **6 minarets**, around it | It has six minarets, which was very unusual when it was built. |
| **20,000 tiles**, inside | More than 20,000 hand-painted tiles from İznik line its walls inside, many of them blue: that is how it got its nickname. |

Istanbul's altitude (39 m) is from WorldAtlas; other references give up to 100 m.

### Hagia Sophia (scale: 55 m, compared with a bus)

| Statistic | Fact |
|---|---|
| **537**, completed | It was completed in 537, after less than six years of building. |
| **55 m**, to the dome | Its great dome rises about 55 metres above the floor. |
| *(none)* | Built as a cathedral, it became a mosque in 1453, a museum in 1934, and a mosque again in 2020. |

The dome collapsed in 558 and was rebuilt higher; 55 m is today's dome.
